# MaintainPro System Demo Guide

Use one scenario from beginning to end: **Street Lighting — Exposed Wiring**. Keep the demo focused on how a community concern becomes completed, reviewed work.

## Before the demo

- Prepare one official account and one active personnel account from the Maintenance crew.
- Have a safe sample image ready for initial evidence and another for completion evidence.
- Open separate browser sessions for the reporter, official, and personnel.
- Use the disposable demo database when rehearsing. Do not reuse a real resident's tracking details.

## 1. Landing page

Show the purpose: **Report and Track Barangay Concerns**. Point out the two main actions, the official category cards, and that guests do not need an account.

Choose **Street Lighting** from the category cards. Confirm that the report form opens with Street Lighting already selected.

## 2. Reporter submits a concern

1. Select **Exposed wiring**.
2. Select relevant key points such as **Sparks visible**, **Exposed wires**, and **Near school** when appropriate for the scenario.
3. Enter the Purok or Sitio, street, exact area, and optional landmark.
4. Add a short description and the initial evidence image.
5. Open the safety guidance briefly, then submit.
6. Save the concern reference and private tracking code.

Explain that the exact location, evidence, and staff notes remain protected.

## 3. Official reviews and assigns

1. Sign in as the official and show the new concern on the dashboard.
2. Open the concern and review its category, location, details, evidence, priority recommendation, and duplicate suggestions.
3. Record the assessment and official recommended action.
4. Assign the prepared personnel account and set a target completion date.

Explain that recommendations support the official decision; they do not automatically change priority, assignment, or status.

## 4. Personnel performs the work

1. Sign in as personnel and open **My Work**.
2. Open the assigned concern and review the instructions.
3. Choose **Start Work**, record the work status and action, and upload progress evidence.
4. Add one progress update.
5. Choose **Complete / resolve work**, record the completed action, and upload completion evidence.

## 5. Official reviews completion

1. Return to the official session.
2. Open the concern awaiting review.
3. Compare the before and after evidence and read the personnel update.
4. Choose **Close Concern** when the evidence is sufficient.

Mention that an official can reopen the concern with instructions when more work is needed.

## 6. Reporter tracks the result

1. Open **Track Concern**.
2. Enter the saved reference and tracking code.
3. Show the final **Closed** status, public progress, and reporter messages.

## Close the demo

Summarize the flow: **Report → Review → Assign → Work → Review → Close → Track**.

Mention that reports, action plans, solution rules, audit history, backups, settings, and other administrative capabilities remain available under **More Tools** and **Administration**. Do not open them during the main scenario unless asked.
